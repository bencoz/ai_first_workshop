# ✅ Module 6: Wrap‑Up & Reflection (30m)

Reflect on your AI‑first journey, share lessons learned, and commit to next steps you’ll adopt as a team.

---

## 🎯 Outcomes

- Internalize AI‑first habits you want to keep
- Capture team commitments and owners for next steps
- Collect feedback and schedule a follow‑up checkpoint

---

## ⏱️ Timeboxed Agenda (30 minutes)

- 3m — Silent self‑assessment
- 10m — Pair/group share‑outs (wins, frictions, artifacts)
- 8m — Commitments and owners (Start/Stop/Continue)
- 6m — Feedback + success metrics
- 3m — Next steps and close

---

## ✍️ Self‑Assessment (3m)

Individually jot answers (bullets):
- Where did AI accelerate you most today?
- What slowed you down or felt risky?
- Which habit will you start using immediately (be specific)?

---

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Share‑Outs (10m)

Structure: 60–90s per pair/team. Show one artifact.
- Wins: a prompt, diff, or doc you’re proud of
- Frictions: one confusion or pitfall (how you’d avoid it next time)
- Artifact tour: 1 screenshot or file (e.g., routes table, story notes)

Prompt the room:
- What worked really well with Copilot/Agent Mode?
- What was frustrating or slow? Any hallucinations caught by verification?
- How will AI fit into your daily workflow next week?

---

## 📝 Commitments (8m)

Use Start/Stop/Continue to agree on 3–5 concrete actions with owners and dates.

Start
- Plan‑first prompts with acceptance criteria before coding
- Check in `*.instructions` and minimal `*.prompt.md` for common tasks
- Attach key docs as Knowledge in VS Code

Stop
- Editing big AI blobs without a plan or review
- Letting prompts sprawl without ownership/versioning

Continue
- Small diffs; “own the merge”; verify against code/tests
- Add lightweight evals/tests for AI‑touched changes

Assign an owner and a target date for each commitment.

---

## 📊 Feedback & Metrics (6m)

- 2m: Pulse survey (thumbs/QR or link): confidence, usefulness, readiness to recommend
- 2m: Define 2 success metrics you’ll track (e.g., PR size, time‑to‑review, test coverage for AI‑touched code)
- 2m: Note topics for a deeper dive (e.g., MCP, retrieval, eval harness)

Example pulse survey questions (1–5):
- Confidence using AI in this stack?
- Did today’s workflows feel practical and safe?
- Would you recommend them to teammates?

---

## 🔮 What’s Next (3m)

- Schedule a 30‑day check‑in to review metrics and adjust practices
- Add a `docs/AI_FIRST_ADOPTION.md` with your 3–5 commitments and owners
- Pick one repo to pilot: add `.copilot-instruction` + `*.prompt.md` + a lightweight eval

---

## 🧑‍🏫 Facilitator Notes

- Keep share‑outs crisp; timebox to maintain energy
- Capture commitments live in a visible doc; confirm owners and dates
- If time runs short, skip artifact tours and prioritize commitments
